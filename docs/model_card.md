# Model card: synthetic frequency-severity pricing baseline

## Intended use

Educational demonstration of a personal-auto frequency-severity pricing workflow and out-of-time model review. Not for quoting, underwriting, claims decisions, or customer treatment.

## Data

Generated by `insurance_pricing.data.make_synthetic_policies`. Contains synthetic policy-year rows for 2021â€“2024 with exposure, driver/vehicle attributes, territory, claim count, aggregate loss, and noisy synthetic written premium. No real personal data is used.

## Models

- Frequency: Poisson regression on claim count per exposure, weighted by exposure.
- Severity: Gamma regression on average loss per claim, weighted by claim count.
- Pure premium: predicted annual frequency multiplied by predicted mean severity.

## Evaluation

Train on 2021â€“2023 and evaluate on 2024. Report Poisson deviance, Gamma deviance, pure-premium MAE/RMSE, and observed-to-expected loss cost by predicted-risk decile. Metrics are regenerated with the fixed random seed and are not claims of production performance.

## Risks and limitations

The synthetic generator encodes simplified risk relationships and only one mild time trend. There is no real exposure, coverage, inflation, deductible, policy tenure, claims development, rate filing, market response, protected-class review, or operational impact. Segment differentials are exploratory and must not be used as recommendations for real pricing. Current premium is synthetic and used only for a capped scenario comparison.

## Human oversight

Any real pricing work requires actuarial ownership, model risk review, legal/compliance review, fairness analysis, approved data governance, documented assumptions, and monitoring. This package does not automate individual pricing decisions.
